📱 Nextcloud Android: Unlimited Auto-Upload Guide
How to continuously backup 5,000+ WhatsApp Images, Videos & Camera Photos in the background without any 500-file limitation.
⚠️ Why does "You can upload up to 500 files at once" appear?
The manual file picker (+ → Upload) has a built-in safety limit of 500 files to protect phone memory from crashing. To upload thousands of files seamlessly, use Auto Upload.
1
Open Nextcloud App & Access Menu
Open the Nextcloud App on your Android phone. Tap the three horizontal lines (☰) in the top-left corner to open the sidebar.
2
Select "Auto upload" (or Media Sync)
Tap "Auto upload" from the sidebar menu. Nextcloud will automatically detect and list all media folders on your device (WhatsApp Images, WhatsApp Video, Camera, Screenshots, Downloads, etc.).
3
Enable Sync for WhatsApp & Camera Folders
Locate WhatsApp Images and tap the Cloud icon (☁️) on the right side. The cloud icon will turn Blue, indicating sync is active.
4
Configure Key Upload Settings (Important)
In the configuration popup that appears, set the following recommended options:
✅ Upload existing files: Check this box so all your existing 5,000+ images upload immediately.
📂 Target folder: Set to /Photos/WhatsApp Images (or your preferred cloud folder).
🔒 Upload option: Select "Keep file in source folder" (preserves your local copies).
📶 When to upload: Select "Only on Wi-Fi" or "Wi-Fi and mobile data" depending on your data plan.
5
Disable Android Battery Optimization for 24/7 Sync
To allow Nextcloud to upload thousands of files in the background without pausing when the screen turns off:
Go to your Phone Settings → Apps → Nextcloud → Battery → Select "Unrestricted" / "No Restrictions".
6
Repeat for Other Media Folders & Verify
Repeat Steps 3 & 4 for WhatsApp Video and Camera (DCIM). You can verify your live uploaded photos anytime at https://anantatechcare.com.
